% ---------- introduction ----------
{\small\setlength{\parskip}{0pt}\color{ink}
Machine-learned interatomic potentials (MLIPs) and force fields simulate proteins, materials, and reactive chemistry at a fraction of the cost of quantum mechanics, but they are fundamentally bounded by their training data.
That data is the bottleneck in AI4Science: gold-standard coupled cluster cannot scale past small molecules, while standard Kohn-Sham DFT (KS-DFT) trades away accuracy.
I attack this bottleneck by rethinking electronic structure modeling itself.
I train equivariant neural exchange-correlation (XC) functionals end-to-end through GPU-accelerated differentiable self-consistent field (SCF) solvers, unlocking hybrid-level quantum chemistry accuracy where hybrids are computationally out of reach.
Then I make these calculations fast.
Learned SCF initializations slash the iterations needed to converge, while direct density-matrix and Hamiltonian prediction bypasses the solver loop entirely.
My goal is to make coupled-cluster-quality reference data cheap enough to generate at the scale modern force fields demand.\par}
