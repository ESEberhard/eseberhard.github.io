% ---------- introduction ----------
{\small\setlength{\parskip}{0pt}\color{ink}
Machine-learned force fields simulate complex chemistry at a fraction of the cost of quantum mechanics, but they are fundamentally bounded by their training data.
That data is the bottleneck: gold-standard coupled cluster cannot scale past small molecules, while standard DFT trades away accuracy.
I attack this bottleneck by rethinking DFT itself.
I train neural exchange-correlation functionals end-to-end through the self-consistent field solver, unlocking hybrid-level accuracy where hybrids are computationally out of reach.
Then I make these calculations fast.
Learned initializations slash the iterations needed to converge, while direct density-matrix prediction bypasses the solver loop entirely.
My goal is to make coupled-cluster-quality data cheap enough to generate at the scale modern force fields demand.\par}
